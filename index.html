Failure 01

Vague catch-all purposes

Failure 01

“Other related activities” excludes nothing. A purpose that rules nothing out is not a purpose.

Failure 02

No way to withdraw consent

Failure 02

The policy says you can. It never says how. No link, no address, no form, anywhere in the document.

Failure 03

No named contact person

Failure 03

info@ is not a person. No name, no designation, no address. Nobody is accountable for the answer.

The twelve-question self-check

Privacy Policy Self-Check
Does it name every category of data you collect?
Is there one stated purpose per category?
Are third parties named, not just described?
Is there a retention period, in months or years?
Can a person withdraw consent, and is the route written down?
Is a named person accountable, with a designation?
Does deletion cover backups and vendors?
Is there a stated response time for requests?
Does it say what happens to children’s data?
Is cross-border transfer disclosed by country?
Is the last-updated date within twelve months?
Could your own engineer follow it without asking you?
